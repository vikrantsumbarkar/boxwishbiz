How to choose employee gifts without making the selection generic.

A strong employee gift balances usefulness, occasion, recipient fit and presentation. The best choice is not always the most expensive product.

A simple framework

Five questions to answer before selecting products

1. Who is receiving it? Employee level, team, role and lifestyle can influence the product choice.

2. Why are you gifting? Onboarding, recognition, festival appreciation, anniversary and events call for different formats.

3. What will they use? Start with practical value, then add branding or personalisation.

4. What needs to be produced? Confirm quantity, branding method, packaging and personalisation before finalising.

5. Where and when? Delivery locations and required dates can change which products and fulfilment methods are practical.

Avoid common mistakes

Don't start with the logo. Start with the recipient.

Over-branding, impractical products and last-minute production can reduce the impact of an otherwise good gift. A clear brief helps your supplier suggest better combinations and flag timeline constraints early.
