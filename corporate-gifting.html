Why a structured gifting process matters

Corporate gifting is more than choosing a product.

For business gifting, the right product is only one part of the brief. Branding, presentation, quantity, recipient profile and delivery requirements all affect the final solution.

01

Start with the recipient

Employee, client, partner, visitor or event attendee — the audience shapes the right gift direction.

02

Match the occasion

Onboarding, milestones, festivals, client relationships and events need different approaches.

03

Build the brand experience

Choose useful products, branding and presentation that feel consistent with your organisation.

04

Plan fulfilment

Quantity, packing and delivery locations should be considered before the order is finalised.
