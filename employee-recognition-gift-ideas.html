Employee appreciation guide

Employee recognition gift ideas that feel personal, useful and well considered.

Recognition gifts work best when the format matches the occasion, recipient and company culture rather than simply carrying a logo.

Start with the occasion

Recognition can cover very different moments.

Use a different gifting approach for performance recognition, work anniversaries, promotions, team milestones, festive appreciation and special achievements. The more specific the occasion, the easier it is to make the gift feel intentional.

For recurring programmes, consider a consistent packaging language with flexible product choices. For one-off recognition, a personalised product or message can add more emotional value.

Gift directions

Build around usefulness and meaning

Personalised Desk Gifts

Name, initials or a short message can make a practical desk item feel more individual.

Premium Utility Gifts

Choose useful tech, travel, lifestyle or office products that recipients can keep using.

Curated Gift Boxes

Combine two or more complementary items with a thoughtful presentation and message.

Milestone Gifts

For anniversaries and major achievements, prioritise a more durable or premium keepsake.

Procurement checklist

Make the programme easy to execute

Before requesting a quote, define recipient count, budget range, personalisation requirements, branding, packaging, delivery locations and required date. If gifts are being distributed individually, confirm address collection and labelling requirements separately.

For ongoing recognition programmes, ask your gifting partner how repeat orders, artwork, stock and personalisation can be handled consistently.
