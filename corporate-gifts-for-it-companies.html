Industry gifting guide

Corporate gifts for IT companies: useful ideas for modern teams.

IT companies often need gifting across employee onboarding, recognition, client relationships, hiring events and conferences. A modular gifting approach makes these requirements easier to manage.

Where gifting fits

One company can have several gifting needs.

New joiners: Build a useful onboarding kit around everyday work and employee experience.

Recognition: Use personalised or premium utility gifts for milestones and achievements.

Clients: Select polished gifts or curated hampers that fit the relationship and occasion.

Events: Create conference kits, event merchandise and giveaways around the audience and event format.

Shortlist categories

Practical gifting directions for technology teams

New Joinee Kits

Combine onboarding essentials, branded merchandise and useful work or lifestyle products.

Branded Merchandise

Choose apparel, bags, bottles, stationery, desk accessories or tech products based on use.

Client Gifts

Use premium presentation and selective branding rather than making every item heavily branded.

Conference Kits

Match the kit to attendee profile, event duration, branding and practical event-day needs.

Build the brief

What to share with your gifting partner

Start with the audience, quantity, budget range, branding method, personalisation, packaging, delivery locations and timeline. For IT companies with recurring hiring or events, mention whether the requirement is one-time or ongoing.
