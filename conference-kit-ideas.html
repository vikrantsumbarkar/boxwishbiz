Conference Kit Ideas: What to Include and How to Plan

A conference kit should be useful during the event, easy to carry and consistent with the event brand. The best kit depends on the audience, event format and timeline.

Start with the event format

Day conferences, multi-day events, internal meetings and large public conferences may need different combinations. Identify what attendees will actually use before selecting products.

Useful conference kit directions

Common options can include a bag or organiser, notebook, pen, bottle, badge holder, event information, technology accessories and other branded essentials.

Keep the branding consistent

Use the event identity consistently across selected products, packaging and printed inserts. Not every item needs a large logo.

Plan the attendee experience

Think about when attendees receive the kit, how they carry it, what they need immediately and what they can keep using after the event.

Allow time for production

Event merchandise often has a fixed deadline. Confirm artwork, product availability, branding method, packaging and delivery schedule before committing to quantities.

For larger events

Share the expected quantity, audience profile, event city, delivery date and branding requirements early. This allows a supplier to recommend options that are realistic for the timeline.
