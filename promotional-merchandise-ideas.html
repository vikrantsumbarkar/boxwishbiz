Merchandise guide

Promotional merchandise ideas people will actually use.

Good promotional merchandise starts with the audience and use case. Choose products that fit the campaign, event, workplace or customer experience instead of branding items simply because they are available.

Plan Promotional MerchandiseExplore Branded Merchandise
Think audience first

Match the product to where it will be used.

For office teams, consider desk accessories, bottles, stationery, bags and useful tech products. For conferences, prioritise items that help attendees during the event and remain useful afterwards. For customer campaigns, select products that fit the audience rather than forcing a generic corporate style.

Branding should support the product. Depending on the item, a subtle logo, print, engraving, label, packaging element or custom insert may create a more premium result than oversized branding.

Procurement checklist

Give your supplier the information needed for a useful quote

Share quantity, target budget, audience, product preferences, branding artwork or method, packaging needs, delivery locations and timeline. If the merchandise is for an event, include the event date and how items will be distributed.
