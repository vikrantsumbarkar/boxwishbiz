Personalised Gift Ideas: How to Choose the Right Custom Gift

Personalisation works best when the custom detail has a reason to be there. Start with the recipient and occasion, then choose a product that suits the person.

Name and initial gifts

Names and initials work well on suitable accessories, stationery, drinkware and keepsakes where the personal detail remains useful and visually clean.

Photo gifts

Photo-led gifts can be meaningful for birthdays, anniversaries, family celebrations and couple gifting. The product should support the photo rather than make it difficult to use or display.

Custom messages and dates

A short message, date or meaningful phrase can make a simple gift feel more considered. Keep the text appropriate to the product and recipient.

Personalised gift boxes

For a larger occasion, combine several suitable gifts into one coordinated box. The selection, packaging and personalisation can be planned together.

Personalised office gifts

For employee or client gifting, personalisation should remain professional. Names, initials or subtle branding can add a personal touch without making the gift feel promotional.

Before confirming an order

Confirm the personalisation method, artwork or spelling, quantity, product availability, packaging and delivery requirements before production.
