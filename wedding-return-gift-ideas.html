Choose the experience

Think beyond a decorative keepsake.

Useful lifestyle products, personalised keepsakes, home items, small gourmet combinations and curated gift boxes can all work depending on the celebration and guest profile.

If the guest list is mixed, choose a broadly useful gift. If the event has a strong theme, colour palette or cultural style, packaging and personalisation can carry that theme without making the product impractical.

Ideas to consider

Four directions for a thoughtful return gift

Personalised Keepsakes

Add names, initials, dates or a short message to a practical or decorative gift.

Useful Home Gifts

Choose items guests can use at home rather than gifts that are purely ceremonial.

Mini Gift Boxes

Combine a few complementary items into one coordinated presentation.

Theme-led Packaging

Use labels, sleeves, boxes, ribbons or inserts to connect the gift to the wedding style.

Planning checklist

Quantity and presentation matter as much as the idea

Confirm the guest quantity, target budget, personalisation details, packaging, delivery location and event date early. For personalised products, allow time for artwork approval and production before the final packing deadline.
